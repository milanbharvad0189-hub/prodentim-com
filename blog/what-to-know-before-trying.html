Before ordering any Oral Probiotics / Dental Health Supplement, it helps to know exactly what you are buying, how it is meant to be used, and what happens if it does not work out. Here is a straight, honest rundown of ProDentim covering each of those points.

What ProDentim Actually Contains

ProDentim lists 7 named ingredients on its label. Here is what each one is included for:

  • Lactobacillus Paracasei — A probiotic bacterium commonly used in oral and dental supplements; included to help populate the mouth with beneficial flora that support gum and tooth comfort.
  • Lactobacillus Reuteri — A well-studied probiotic strain frequently found in mouth and gut formulas, chosen to help maintain a balanced oral microbiome and support healthy gum tissue.
  • Bifidobacterium Lactis — A hardy probiotic species included to complement the Lactobacillus strains and help sustain a favorable bacterial environment in the mouth.
  • Inulin — A soluble plant fiber that acts as food for the probiotic strains, helping them establish and thrive after the tablet is chewed.
  • Malic Acid — A naturally occurring acid used in oral-care products to help maintain a favorable pH balance in the mouth and support a clean-feeling smile.
  • Tricalcium Phosphate — A calcium-phosphate mineral often used in dental products to help support the mineral content and surface strength of teeth.
  • Peppermint — Provides the chewable tablet's cooling mint flavor while helping the mouth feel clean and refreshed after each use.

How It Is Meant To Be Used

The label calls for 1 chewable tablet per day, with 30 tablets in each bottle. Some ingredient amounts are listed as part of a combined blend rather than individually. Consistency day to day matters more than any single dose.

How It Is Meant To Work

ProDentim works by seeding the mouth with live Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium strains, feeding them with inulin, and supporting the tooth surface with tricalcium phosphate so the oral environment stays balanced.

Chew, don't swallow: The tablet is chewed slowly so the probiotic strains and minerals spread across the teeth, gums and cheeks instead of passing straight to the stomach.

Crowd out the bad bacteria: By introducing beneficial Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium strains, the formula aims to shift the balance of the mouth's microbiome in a healthier direction.

Feed the good strains: Inulin acts as a prebiotic, giving the newly introduced bacteria a food source so they can settle in rather than wash away.

Support the tooth surface: Tricalcium phosphate and malic acid are included to help maintain the mineral environment and pH balance that keep teeth feeling clean and strong.
