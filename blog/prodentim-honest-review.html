ProDentim is a chewable dietary supplement built around the idea that a healthy mouth depends on a healthy balance of bacteria. Instead of relying on brushing and flossing alone, it delivers a blend of probiotic strains plus select nutrients directly to the oral environment, aiming to support teeth and gums from the inside out. Each tablet is meant to be chewed slowly so the ingredients can coat the mouth, and the formula is positioned as part of a daily oral-care routine rather than a replacement for it.

Who Makes ProDentim, And What It Is Not

ProDentim is not a toothpaste or a replacement for dental visits — it's a chewable probiotic supplement meant to sit alongside your normal brushing and flossing routine. That distinction matters more than marketing copy — it shapes what a reasonable buyer should expect from a Oral Probiotics / Dental Health Supplement like this one.

What ProDentim Claims To Support

  • Supports a balanced oral microbiome — delivers live probiotic strains that aim to keep the mouth's bacterial mix healthy and stable.
  • Promotes healthy-looking gums — the Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium strains are chosen to help gums stay comfortable and firm.
  • Helps maintain clean-feeling teeth — tricalcium phosphate and malic acid work with brushing to support a fresh, smooth smile.
  • Freshens breath from within — peppermint plus a balanced bacterial environment target the source of everyday mouth odor.
  • Complements your daily oral routine — a chewable format that fits alongside brushing and flossing as an extra layer of support.
  • Easy to take consistently — a pleasant mint chewable tablet is simple to work into mornings or evenings without water.
